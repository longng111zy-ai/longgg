## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the timeless elegance, curated tactility, and cultural depth of bespoke, high-end souvenirs and artisanal masterworks. The digital experience mirrors stepping into an intimate heritage gallery or an architectural atelier: quiet, composed, and distinctly luxurious.

### Brand Narrative & Emotional Core
- **Curated Provenance:** Every crafted item carries cultural memory, intentional material choices, and the hand of master artisans. The interface recedes gracefully, framing objects with gallery-grade focus.
- **Warm Architectural Modernism:** Rather than cold starkness or rustic cliches, the visual language balances warm earthy pigments (weathered taupe, slate brown, and deep charcoal) with generous, museum-grade negative space.
- **Sensory Tactility:** Subtle transitions, soft low-contrast keylines reminiscent of handmade paper edges, and ambient warmth in shadows evoke textures of unglazed ceramics, silk weaves, polished lacquer, and stone.

### Design Movement: Warm Minimalist Editorial
The interface integrates Scandinavian editorial layout principles with the warm, contemplative restraint of traditional Eastern craftsmanship. Surfaces remain predominantly flat with subtle tonal shifts, delicate 0.5px–1px structural hairlines, and quiet ambient layering.

## Colors

The color palette is derived directly from raw materials: weathered stone, aged timber, raw linen, and brushed metal. It avoids sterile blue-tinted whites in favor of radiant, creamy ivory and neutral earthen grounds.

### Role & Application Guidelines
- **Primary (`#86736D` - Weathered Taupe):** Serves as the primary anchor for dominant headlines, primary call-to-actions, high-contrast borders, and critical typography. Conveys weight, authority, and permanence.
- **Secondary (`#7F7572` - Muted Slate Brown):** Represents the warmth of kiln-fired earth and stone. Used for active badges, hover state transitions, thematic tags, and editorial accents.
- **Tertiary (`#192322` - Deep Charcoal Accent):** Used sparingly to denote provenance, certification badges, price points, and premium finishes. Never used for long-form text.
- **Neutral Surface Palette:**
  - `Canvas Ground (`#7A7675` / Lightened variants):` The global foundation. Softens high contrast, creating a warm canvas.
  - `Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Cream Gesso):` Applied to product cards, floating panels, and drawers to create soft architectural elevation.
  - `Surface Subdued (`#EFE8DE` - Pressed Oat):` Background for chips, search bars, table headers, and inactive states.
  - `Hairline Border (`#E2D8CA` - Raw Silk Border):` Used for structural grid divisions and perimeter outlines.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text is set in `#86736D`, secondary supporting metadata in `#7F7572`, and placeholder text in `#9E8E84`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the editorial grace of `Playfair Display` with the balanced, modern rhythm of `Plus Jakarta Sans`.

### Typographic Rationale & Principles
- **Headings & Display:** `Playfair Display` provides literary depth, evocative ligatures, and classic proportions. Titles use regular to medium weights with tight letter-spacing to avoid a loud or heavy commercial demeanor.
- **Body & Captions:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` delivers crisp legibility across dense artisanal stories, technical specifications, provenance certificates, and commerce interfaces. Generous line-height (`1.6` to `1.7`) promotes relaxed reading.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** All category indicators, edition numbers, and SKU labels leverage uppercase `label-sm` or `label-md` with expanded letter-spacing (`+0.06em` to `+0.08em`) to mirror luxury museum placards.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system operates on an 8pt base grid wrapped within generous boundary buffers. It utilizes rhythmic negative space to preserve the museum-like pacing of the boutique experience.

### Grid & Breakpoint Strategy
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout. Margin set to `margin-lg` (5rem/80px), gutters set to `gutter-lg` (3rem/48px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1440px` to maintain focused visual lines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout. Margin set to `margin` (3rem/48px), gutters to `gutter` (2rem/32px). Product grids reflow gracefully from 3-up to 2-up configurations.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout. Margin reduced to `margin-sm` (1.25rem/20px), gutters to `gutter-sm` (1rem/16px). Grids collapse to single-column or offset horizontal swipe rails for featured collections.

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is treated as an active design element. Structural sections must breathe with minimum vertical spacing intervals of `space-xl` (48px) to double-XL (96px). Crowding artifacts, pricing, or story narratives compromises the perceived value of the artisanal items.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm ambient lighting, tonal surface layering, and micro-thin borders rather than artificial digital dropshadows.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Base Tier (Ground Canvas):** Matte `#F9F6F0` (Bleached Linen). Completely flat, non-reflective backdrop.
2. **Surface Tier (Cards, Modules, Flyouts):** Pristine `#FFFFFF` surfaced with a precise `1px solid #E2D8CA` border.
3. **Ambient Warmth (Resting Elevation):**
   - Soft, diffused shadow tinted with warm umber: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(134, 115, 109, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(134, 115, 109, 0.02)`.
4. **Interactive Hover Lift:**
   - Elevates subtly: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(134, 115, 109, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(134, 115, 109, 0.03)`.
   - Subtle scale translation (`translateY(-2px)`) with standard ease curve (`cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` over 300ms).
5. **Overlays & Modals:**
   - Backdrops implement a warm muted diffusion: `rgba(134, 115, 109, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`.
   - Elevated sheet uses high-altitude diffuse shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(134, 115, 109, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances architectural discipline with soft, tactile refinement. 

### Corner Geometry
A soft corner curvature (`roundedness: 1`) is applied throughout the system:
- **Base Components (Buttons, Input Fields, Badges):** Subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This avoids mechanical sharpness while remaining structured and bespoke.
- **Intermediate Containers (Product Cards, Popovers):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius to frame photography with gentle corners.
- **Expanded Surfaces (Modals, Feature Drawers):** `0.75rem` (12px) radius.
- **Full Radius (Pill Form):** Reserved solely for contextual tags, curation pills, and count badges to balance the prevailing rectilinear architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#86736D` (Weathered Taupe) with text in `#F9F6F0`. Micro-radius of `4px`, padding `14px 28px`. Hover shifts smoothly to `#7F7572` (Muted Slate Brown). Active state applies a subtle `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #86736D`, text `#86736D`. Hover transitions background to `#EFE8DE` with zero border jitter.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Understated label in `#86736D` paired with an animated directional underline that expands from left to right on hover using `#7F7572`.

### Chips & Filters
- **Pill Shape:** Enclosed in a `24px` radius.
- **Default State:** Transparent background, `1px solid #E2D8CA`, text in `#7F7572`.
- **Selected State:** Background in `#86736D`, text in `#FFFFFF`, border in `#86736D`. 
- **Sub-tag Variant:** Muted Slate Brown tint (`#7F7572` at 10% opacity) with slate brown text for artisanal provenance tags (e.g., "Hand-Thrown", "Limited Edition").

### Cards (Artisan & Product)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with a crisp `1px solid #E2D8CA` border.
- Image container features an intentional 4:5 vertical editorial ratio, rendered with an inner bleed and a warm neutral placeholder background (`#EFE8DE`).
- Content padding: `space-lg` (24px) for title, maker lineage, pricing, and material tags.
- Transitions: Elevation shifts from resting warm diffusion to hover lift smoothly with zero displacement of inner layout coordinates.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Crisp bottom-line emphasis or full enclosed box with `1px solid #E2D8CA` on `#FFFFFF` ground. Padding `12px 16px`. Active focus shifts border to `#86736D` accompanied by an imperceptible `2px` focus halo in `#192322` (20% opacity).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimal custom glyphs with `#86736D` fill and crisp ivory tick marks. Never default to browser UI controls.

### Curated Collection Lists
- Separated by soft horizontal dividers (`1px solid #E2D8CA`).
- Uses generous vertical padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`).
- Includes metadata tags (origin, year, artisan name) set in `label-sm` with charcoal (`#192322`) accents.

### Specialized Components
- **Certificate of Authenticity Plaque:** A framed container utilizing `#F9F6F0` surface, double hairline borders in `#192322`, and centered serif typography to display maker signature and edition numbering.
- **Materiality Swatch Selector:** Miniature circular swatches with active selection designated by a concentric double ring (`#86736D` outer hairline, `2px` offset white void).
